Frequently Asked Questions

Find answers to common NOVAGRID questions.

What is NOVAGRID?

NOVAGRID is a hosting platform where customers can purchase and manage hosting services.

How do I purchase a hosting plan?

Choose a plan from the Hosting Plans page. After the billing system is connected, you will be able to complete the purchase through the available payment methods.

Can I manage my hosting myself?

Yes. The future client dashboard will allow customers to manage their hosting services and account.

How can I contact support?

You can use the Support page to create a support ticket. The support team can then reply to your ticket.

Do you support Discord login?

Discord login will be connected to the account system later. The current button is only a visual placeholder.

How can I check server status?

Visit the Hosting Status page to see the current status of NOVAGRID services.

What payment methods will be available?

Payment options will be configured by the NOVAGRID administrator. This can include supported online payment methods and, if desired, manual payment verification.

Can I request a refund?

Refund availability will depend on the final NOVAGRID refund policy. Customers can contact support regarding billing questions.
